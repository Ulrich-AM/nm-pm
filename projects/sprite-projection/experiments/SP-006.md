# SP-006 - Rigid Geometry Generalization Stress Test

**Phase:** 5  
**Harness build:** 1.7.0  
**Status:** Implemented, native results pending

## Question

Do the reconstruction methods developed using A1 generalize to rigid objects whose silhouettes and visibility patterns differ substantially from the design subject?

## Frozen baseline

A1 remains the original design subject. Phase 2 through Phase 4 reconstruction formulas are not retuned before SP-006 is executed.

Methods:

- NEAR
- STRETCH
- SHEAR
- AFFINE
- DUAL
- GRID4

## Stress subjects

### A2 - protrusion

Long off-center appendages and a depth-facing spur. Intended to amplify view-dependent silhouette changes.

### A3 - thin structure

Narrow rods and slabs. Intended to expose boundary error, disappearing features, and instability in blending.

### A4 - concavity

A U-shaped body with a negative-space opening. Intended to expose reconstruction errors when holes and concave silhouettes appear or disappear.

### A5 - occlusion

Overlapping front and rear components at different depths. Intended to expose visibility-order and self-occlusion changes.

## Capture policy

Each subject receives the same bank structure used by A1:

- yaw: 0, 45, 90, 135, 180, 225, 270, 315 degrees
- pitch: -60, -30, 0, +30, +60 degrees
- roll: reconstructed in screen space
- capture size: 128 x 128

No subject receives a denser bank merely because it is harder.

## E5 protocol

For A1 through A5:

- yaw sampled every 15 degrees
- pitch sampled every 15 degrees from -60 through +60 degrees
- 216 orientations per subject
- all six reconstruction methods
- silhouette IoU
- symmetric boundary error
- centroid displacement

E5 exports:

- `results/phase5/E5_rigid_stress_metrics.csv`
- `results/phase5/E5_rigid_stress_summary.csv`

The summary reports each method's mean metrics for each subject and its IoU change relative to A1.

## Interpretation rule

SP-006 does not define a universal acceptable-error threshold before measurement. Generalization is characterized through relative degradation, cross-subject ranking, and visual failure cases.

## Promotion relevance

A reconstruction method cannot satisfy the study's promotion rule using A1 alone. SP-006 supplies the required independent rigid-object confirmation step, but articulation remains a later test.
