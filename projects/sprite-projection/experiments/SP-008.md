# SP-008 - Three-Link Directional Sprite Chain

**Phase:** 7  
**Harness build:** 1.9.0  
**Status:** Implemented, native visual and quantitative results pending

## Question

Can three independently reconstructed directional sprites remain visually coherent when two hinges, three part orientations, and repeated depth-order changes are introduced?

## Motivation

SP-007 showed that a two-link articulated object can be represented as two pivot-registered sprite parts. The next controlled step is a three-link chain before any simplified humanoid body is attempted.

This adds:

- a second hinge;
- a third independent sprite bank;
- more frequent depth-order changes;
- more possible overlap combinations;
- compounding orientation error across the chain.

## Roll correction inherited from SP-007 review

A user-provided Phase 6 screenshot exposed a screen-space roll sign mismatch.

The mathematical convention uses Y-up coordinates and projects to a Y-down screen. raylib texture rotation uses the opposite visible sign. The shared reconstruction layer now converts mathematical roll to the opposite-sign raylib texture rotation.

The correction is centralized in `calibration_math.hpp` and `sprite_reconstruction.hpp`, so rigid and articulated experiments use the same convention.

## Rig

SP-008 contains:

- upper link;
- middle link;
- lower link;
- root orientation;
- hinge A;
- hinge B.

The two hinge axes are intentionally different diagonal axes in local XZ. This increases the number of view and depth transitions compared with a planar chain.

## Sprite banks

Each part receives an independent 40-view bank:

- 8 yaw samples;
- 5 pitch samples;
- 128 x 128 capture;
- roll reconstructed in screen space;
- capture pivot fixed at the texture center.

The three banks use unique identifiers:

- `chain-upper`
- `chain-middle`
- `chain-lower`

## Composition

At runtime:

1. true 3D forward kinematics produces all three part poses;
2. each part independently selects/reconstructs its directional sprite;
3. the sprite texture center is placed at the projected proximal joint;
4. complete part sprites are sorted by 3D center depth;
5. the three reconstructed sprites are composed into one articulated approximation.

Center-depth ordering remains intentionally simple and is expected to fail for some interpenetrating overlap cases.

The detailed export also records which link center is closest to the camera for each pose.

## Promotion relevance

SP-008 is the final mechanical articulation checkpoint before a simplified full-body subject.

A reconstruction method that only succeeds on one- or two-link subjects should not be promoted into the final reusable reference implementation.

## Next phase

If SP-008 is usable, Phase 8 should construct a simplified articulated body using the same part-bank, pivot-registration, and depth-aware composition rules. The body should remain deliberately simple enough that failures can still be diagnosed part by part.
