# SP-010 - Final Reference Rig and Mixed-Density Sprite Banks

**Phase:** 9  
**Harness build:** 3.0.0  
**Status:** Implemented, native visual and E9 results pending

## Question

Can the complete Sprite Projection pipeline support a more production-like articulated body while reducing sprite density for small terminal parts without changing the core reconstruction method?

## Final rig

The final body contains 16 rendered parts:

- head + neck;
- upper body;
- lower body;
- hips;
- left/right upper arms;
- left/right lower arms;
- left/right hands;
- left/right upper legs;
- left/right lower legs;
- left/right feet.

The neck remains a logical retarget joint but is visually merged into the head bank.

## Unique bank policy

Symmetric left/right limbs reuse the same bank.

Ten unique part banks are required:

1. head + neck;
2. upper body;
3. lower body;
4. hips;
5. upper arm;
6. lower arm;
7. hand;
8. upper leg;
9. lower leg;
10. foot.

Eight banks retain the standard sampling policy:

- yaw: 0,45,90,135,180,225,270,315 degrees;
- pitch: -60,-30,0,+30,+60 degrees;
- 40 sprites per bank.

Hands and feet use a deliberately sparse policy:

- yaw: 0,90,180,270 degrees;
- pitch: -60,-30,0,+30,+60 degrees;
- 20 sprites per bank.

The resulting maximum authored workload is:

```text
8 full banks × 40 = 320
2 sparse banks × 20 = 40
total unique sprites = 360
```

## Sparse-bank implementation

Sparse yaw is implemented in the shared directional-bank layer rather than as an exporter-only exception.

Each bank records its yaw count. Neighbor selection, DUAL blending, GRID4 blending, baseline residual handling, capture generation, disk loading, and runtime placement all use the bank's own yaw spacing.

Existing eight-view experiments keep the original behavior through the default yaw count of eight.

## FBX retargeting

The final FBX bridge adds logical joints for:

- spine;
- neck;
- hand tips;
- toe endpoints.

Spine, neck, hand-tip, and toe helpers are optional during import.

When absent:

- spine is synthesized between pelvis and chest;
- neck is synthesized between chest and head;
- hand direction continues from the lower arm;
- foot direction uses a forward-biased continuation from the lower leg.

Required major joints still fail explicitly if they cannot be mapped.

## E9 protocol

E9 evaluates the active final-rig animation source.

Temporal samples:

- 18 evenly spaced points.

View samples:

- yaw: 0,90,180,270 degrees;
- pitch: -20,0,+20 degrees.

This produces 216 final-body poses before reconstruction-method multiplication.

All six existing methods remain available:

- NEAR;
- STRETCH;
- SHEAR;
- AFFINE;
- DUAL;
- GRID4.

Metrics:

- silhouette IoU;
- symmetric boundary error;
- centroid displacement.

Outputs:

- `results/phase9/E9_final_rig_metrics.csv`
- `results/phase9/E9_final_rig_summary.csv`

## Finalization rule

SP-010 freezes the reference-candidate architecture.

No reconstruction method is declared universally superior merely because it is the default in the UI. Final paper conclusions must be based on reviewed experiment exports and observed failure conditions.

The final reusable implementation therefore consists of:

- known mathematical 3D pose;
- per-part directional sprite banks;
- per-bank yaw density;
- common five-row pitch sampling;
- screen-space roll;
- pivot registration;
- depth-aware whole-part composition;
- optional FBX animation input terminated at the retarget boundary;
- reproducible sprite-sheet export and manifests.
