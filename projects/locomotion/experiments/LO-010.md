## Attribution

LO-010 is a **published-method reconstruction** of **KLAW** (Keyframe-Less Animation of Walking) by **Armin Bruderlin and Thomas W. Calvert** (SIGGRAPH 1989).

NM-PM does **not** claim authorship of KLAW.

Primary source:

- Bruderlin, A. & Calvert, T. W. (1989). Goal-Directed, Dynamic Animation of Human Walking. *Computer Graphics* 23(3), 233–242.  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/74333.74357

Secondary clarification (not a replacement of 1989 dynamics):

- Bruderlin, A. & Calvert, T. (1993). Interactive Animation of Personalized Human Locomotion. Graphics Interface '93.

Thesis (collision detail only):

This is **not** claimed to be an exact original KLAW implementation.

## Why the study pivoted

LO-001 through LO-009 formed an independent bottom-up exploration of anatomically constrained procedural locomotion. Despite continuous state, articulated support, and stance dynamics, visual evaluation continued to read as alternating steps rather than one flowing walk.

Literature review found substantial overlap with mechanisms already present in KLAW (1989). LO-010 therefore reconstructs that published methodology as an explicit external baseline before further NM-PM modification.

## History of this reconstruction

### First pass — `locomotion-v0.10.0` (too approximate)

The first Phase 9 implementation was structurally KLAW-like and its finite tests passed, but visual QA showed:

- severe snapping between steps;
- recurrent inverted / implausible knees;
- motion unlike the published figures.

Root cause was architectural approximation, not missing polish:

- arbitrary per-step initial conditions (`w0`, `θ4₀`, zero rates);
- conveyor `rootY += v·dt` double-translating the body;
- simplified / “Lagrange-inspired” stance ODE instead of Appendix A.1;
- sine-shaped Swing 1 clearance instead of the published quartic P;
- generic two-link stance IK instead of Figure 6 foot-resolved geometry;
- nearest-sample trajectory playback;
- one-frame Swing 2 torque boost instead of clearance re-simulation.

That failure is preserved in release history. Do not treat “tests green” as fidelity.

### Fidelity pass — `locomotion-v0.10.1`

This revision replaces incorrect Phase 9 internals while preserving Phase 9 file location, harness, KLAW attribution, and LO-001–LO-009 experiments.

## What LO-010 reconstructs (fidelity pass)

- High-level parameters `v`, `sl`, `sf` with equation (1) normalization  
- Step-oriented control (double + single support per step)  
- State-phase timing including frequency-dependent double support  
- Persistent `KlawDynamicBoundaryState` with heel-strike / toe-off inheritance  
- Appendix A.1 stance `A(q) q̈ = B` with eq (4) `Fw` only  
- Appendix A.2 swing driven by determinant-adjusted stance hip path  
- Deterministic implicit trapezoidal integrator (documented LSODE substitute)  
- Event-driven telescopic / locked stance subphases  
- Figure 6 virtual-leg reconstruction with geometric foot pivots  
- Swing 1 quartic P (`C ≈ 0.30·D`) with geometric θ4 feedback  
- Swing 2 clearance retry via incremented `pa` re-simulation  
- Swing 3 hip-torque terminal BVP for heel strike at `t3`  
- Bisection → secant BVP with warm-start  
- Determinants **after** stance **before** swing  
- Continuous trajectory sample interpolation  
- Step-boundary continuity diagnostics (collision discontinuities separate)  
- Paper-compare harness overlays (θ1…θ6, pivots, subphases, virtual vs anatomical)

## What LO-010 deliberately does not do

- Does not replace 1989 dynamics with the 1993 kinematic rewrite  
- Does not reuse LO-009 Model C as “KLAW”  
- Does not modernize into continuous no-reset KLAW (future LO-012)  
- Does not claim natural walking is solved  
- Does not claim exact thesis C.3 collision algebra (ASSUMED soft transfer)
