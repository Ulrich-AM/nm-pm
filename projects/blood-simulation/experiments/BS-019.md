# BS-019 - Impact-Fed Gravity Rivulets

**Phase:** 8 extension  
**Status:** Implemented  
**Milestone:** `blood-simulation-v0.9.0`  
**Date:** 2026-10-20

## Question

Can a fresh dense wall impact form a long gravity-driven rivulet immediately, while preserving the Phase 7 rule that most attached blood remains stationary?

## Motivation

The frozen Phase 7 reference contains two distinct wall behaviors: short impact streaks and the rarer full reservoir runner. The full runner already reaches the gravity-facing edge, matures into a hanging tip, and enters the XYZ droplet solver, but its periodic dense-source nomination can make fresh wall runoff appear absent or delayed.

Phase 8 tests a small extension rather than a new fluid model: a sufficiently wet and dense fresh impact may immediately nominate the existing full-runner path.

## Method

After impact morphology is deposited into the sparse surface field:

1. Phase 8 checks whether the impacted surface carries meaningful projected gravity.
2. Small impacts, mist, body-only material, frozen cells, and insufficiently wet material are rejected.
3. A deterministic local search selects a dense wet candidate near the impact, preferring material farther in the gravity-facing direction while penalizing lateral distance.
4. Previously seeded neighborhoods and nearby active runners are excluded.
5. The accepted candidate gathers the existing bounded Phase 6 runner reservoir.
6. The runner receives a trail budget from its remaining distance to the gravity-facing surface edge.
7. The existing runner update moves the packet under projected world gravity and leaves a narrow persistent trail.
8. At the surface edge, the existing hanging-tip stage and world-space droplet solver take over.
9. The swept XYZ collision path determines the first valid lower surface on which the detached drop lands.

The Phase 7 zero-to-two short impact streaks remain unchanged and may coexist with the long rivulet.

## Safeguards

- one immediate long rivulet may be nominated by an impact;
- dense-zone and wetness thresholds prevent mist from becoming runoff;
- the existing active-runner cap remains in force;
- the six-cell seeded neighborhood prevents repeated emission from the same local region;
- source material is removed from the field before entering the transient runner reservoir;
- all later trail, hanging-tip, airborne, and landing transitions reuse the existing conservative pipeline;
- Phase 3 field-wide wall advection remains superseded and is not reintroduced.

## Success criteria

BS-019 is successful if:

- a qualifying dense wet impact can immediately create one long runner;
- body-only or mist-scale material cannot create a rivulet;
- capture conserves attached field amount plus runner reservoir;
- the runner advances toward lower world Z;
- the runner reaches the gravity-facing edge and enters the hanging/drop path;
- existing Phase 7 short-streak behavior remains available;
- the same seeded region cannot repeatedly emit runners;
- all Phase 0 through Phase 7 regression tests remain valid.
