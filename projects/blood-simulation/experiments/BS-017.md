# BS-017 - Final Scale Sweep and Architecture Freeze

**Phase:** 7  
**Status:** Implemented  
**Milestone:** `blood-simulation-v0.8.0`  
**Date:** 2026-10-20

## Question

Is the hybrid blood method stable enough to freeze as a reusable reference for later NM-PM studies?

## Scope

The final checkpoint does not attempt to turn the study into a general fluid solver. It evaluates the architecture that emerged from the earlier phases:

- true-XYZ free-flight droplets;
- persistent sparse surface-local blood fields;
- deterministic procedural impact morphology;
- dense-region source selection;
- localized reservoir runners for full drips;
- sparse short impact streaks;
- hanging-tip maturation;
- radial floor pooling;
- contact pickup and smearing;
- wetness, age, drying, and freezing;
- direct occupied-cell rendering;
- camera-independent simulation state.

## Scale sweep

The E7 export begins with sixteen deterministic impacts across representative wall and box surfaces and simulates 45 seconds at the fixed 120 Hz movement step.

The export records:

- wet and frozen cell counts;
- allocated sparse tiles;
- estimated field bytes;
- total field amount;
- active full runners;
- active short streaks;
- hanging tips;
- airborne droplets;
- puddle cells;
- runner and streak movement;
- pool and drying maintenance passes;
- detachments and landings.

The final regression suite also keeps transient streak count bounded and checks that a ten-second native update does not produce non-finite state.

## Final reference boundary

The method is considered complete for its intended scope when:

1. persistent blood remains surface-local and independent of camera orientation;
2. world-space particles exist only while material is genuinely airborne;
3. impact, streak, runner, pooling, drying, and transfer paths conserve or explicitly account for material;
4. expensive field-wide maintenance is decoupled from the 120 Hz movement step;
5. transient objects have explicit caps;
6. deterministic seeds reproduce procedural behavior;
7. previous phase harnesses remain buildable as preserved research checkpoints;
8. documentation records superseded approaches rather than deleting them.

## Known limitations retained at freeze

The reference architecture is intentionally not promoted beyond evidence collected in this study.

- Static surface topology remains based on planar rectangular patches and exact edge relationships.
- Arbitrary polygonal and deforming blood-bearing meshes are not validated.
- Pooling is a radial pressure-relaxation approximation rather than a free-surface solver.
- World droplets use simple spherical packets without aerodynamic breakup.
- Surface roughness, porosity, and absorption are not generalized into a full material model.
- Contact transfer remains simplified compared with rigid-body contact manifolds.

These are extension points for later work, not unfinished requirements of the frozen 2.5D method.
