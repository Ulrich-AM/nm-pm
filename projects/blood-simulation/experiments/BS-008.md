# BS-008 - Connected Transfer and Contact Smearing

**Phase:** 4  
**Status:** Implemented  
**Milestone:** `blood-simulation-v0.5.0`  
**Date:** 2026-10-19

## Question

Can persistent blood state move coherently between connected surfaces and transfer through character contact without creating separate decal systems?

## Connected dynamic transfer

Phase 2 introduced exact shared-edge topology for static deposit wrapping.

Phase 4 reuses that topology for active drip channels.

When a drip channel reaches a linked edge:

1. the outgoing point is resolved through the shared world-space edge;
2. the target point is converted into neighbor-local coordinates;
3. transfer is permitted if the target does not move materially upward against gravity;
4. the same drip source continues in the target surface field.

This permits dynamic continuation through compatible connected planar faces.

## Partial support bridge

Not every physical contact is an exact full shared edge.

A downward support probe acts as a limited bridge for common support relationships such as:

- box side → room floor;
- ledge edge → supporting horizontal surface.

This is intentionally narrower than arbitrary mesh adjacency.

## Contact pickup

The player samples wet blood from the upward-facing room floor beneath their current position.

A small fraction is removed from nearby wet cells and stored as carried blood.

Pickup is bounded so crossing a puddle does not erase it.

## Contact deposition

As the player moves, traveled distance accumulates.

When enough carried blood and movement distance are present, a short directional smear is deposited into the same persistent field.

The smear:

- follows the dominant local movement direction;
- uses a short weighted kernel;
- refreshes wetness;
- consumes carried blood.

The system therefore models:

```text
wet floor deposit
      ↓
contact pickup
      ↓
carried amount
      ↓
movement
      ↓
directional smear
```

without introducing a separate footprint texture library.

## Scope boundary

Phase 4 contact transfer currently operates on the main upward-facing floor.

It does not yet model:

- hands touching walls;
- arbitrary rigid-body contact patches;
- deforming character surfaces;
- shoe sole geometry;
- pressure-dependent transfer.

Those are possible later extensions after the surface representation is generalized.

## Success criteria

BS-008 is successful if:

- gravity-driven material can cross compatible connected surface edges;
- physically supported partial edges can transfer into a floor;
- a moving character can pick up wet floor blood;
- carried blood leaves short directional marks;
- transfer remains part of the same persistent field representation.
