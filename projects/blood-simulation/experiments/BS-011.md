# BS-011 - Dense-Region Outlet Gating and Visual Zoning

**Phase:** 5  
**Status:** Implemented  
**Milestone:** `blood-simulation-v0.6.2`  
**Date:** 2026-10-20

## Question

Can drip formation be limited to a small number of outlets on the densest gravity-facing regions of a stain while low-density mist and decorative spray remain non-dripping?

## Motivation

The v0.6.1 source threshold patch made wall drips reliable, but it also allowed too many independent cells to compete as sources. The resulting stain could produce many small runs distributed across the splash instead of a few visually dominant drips emerging from its heavy core.

The field renderer also used one continuous opacity curve for mist, body material, and dense cores. Thin trails could therefore appear too translucent while the dense core lacked enough visual separation from the surrounding spray.

## Method

Phase 5.2 classifies active material into three rendering and source-selection zones:

- Mist: low amount, decorative only, never eligible to start a drip.
- Body: intermediate amount, rendered substantially more opaque but not eligible to start a new source.
- Dense: high amount and sufficient wetness, eligible for dense-region outlet selection.

Dense cells are grouped into connected components with eight-neighbor connectivity. For each component:

1. projected world gravity is expressed in the surface's local U/V basis;
2. only cells on the lower gravity-facing portion of the component are considered;
3. downhill contrast, local peak strength, amount, wetness, and lower-edge position are scored;
4. candidates are sorted deterministically;
5. only one or two well-spaced outlets may be selected from the component;
6. per-surface source caps and regional cooldowns remain active.

The underlying blood field remains continuous. Zone classification changes source eligibility and rendering only.

## Rendering

Opacity now uses a zone-aware nonlinear curve.

- Mist remains faint.
- Body material is substantially more opaque so narrow runs remain visible.
- Dense material is nearly opaque and darker red.
- Hanging tips are also darkened to visually match the dense source region.

## Success criteria

BS-011 is successful if:

- a connected low-density mist region creates zero drip sources;
- one connected dense region creates at least one but no more than two sources;
- selected outlets lie on the lower gravity-facing edge of the dense component;
- selected outlets respect minimum spacing;
- dense opacity is greater than body opacity, which is greater than mist opacity;
- the existing downward-channel, hanging-tip, pooling, transfer, and camera-independence tests still pass.
