# BS-009 - Hanging Drip-Tip Maturation

**Phase:** 5  
**Status:** Implemented  
**Milestone:** `blood-simulation-v0.6.0`  
**Date:** 2026-10-20

## Question

Can unsupported sparse drip channels read more like liquid detachment if a short hanging-bead stage is inserted before the packet becomes a ballistic world-space droplet?

## Motivation

Phase 4 correctly reduced wall flow to a few narrow channels, but an unsupported endpoint still changed from surface material to a free particle almost immediately. The transition was mechanically correct but visually abrupt.

## Method

Phase 5 intercepts newly detached Phase 4 packets and stores them temporarily as hanging tips.

Each tip retains:

- world-space endpoint position;
- release velocity;
- packet volume;
- final droplet radius;
- deterministic seed;
- age and release age.

The visible bead begins smaller than the final droplet radius and grows smoothly over the maturation interval. Larger packets tend to mature somewhat faster. Small deterministic timing variation prevents synchronized detachment.

After maturation, the tip becomes the ordinary Phase 3/4 `WorldDroplet` and continues through the existing gravity and swept-collision pipeline.

## Scope boundary

The endpoint reservoir is a visual and temporal staging approximation. After the packet becomes a hanging tip it does not yet continue drawing additional field mass from the channel.

## Success criteria

BS-009 is successful if:

- an unsupported channel forms a hanging tip before free flight;
- the tip persists for a nonzero deterministic interval;
- the displayed bead grows during that interval;
- release preserves the packet's world position, volume, radius, seed, and velocity;
- hanging tips respect a hard active-count limit;
- the existing world-droplet solver remains unchanged after release.
