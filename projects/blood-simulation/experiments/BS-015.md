# BS-015 - Active-Field Performance Pass

**Phase:** 6  
**Status:** Implemented  
**Milestone:** `blood-simulation-v0.7.1`  
**Date:** 2026-10-20

## Question

Can the Phase 6 lab retain persistent blood while reducing the rapid performance loss caused by repeated field scans and per-cell render bookkeeping?

## Motivation

Persistent deposits grow monotonically during a long lab session. The v0.7.0 renderer built an expanded hash-set of candidate cells around every occupied cell on every frame, while pool relaxation, drying, and puddle statistics repeatedly scanned sparse field tiles at the fixed 120 Hz physics rate.

These operations became increasingly expensive as the number of blood cells grew.

## Changes

### Direct occupied-cell rendering

The renderer now iterates allocated sparse tiles directly and evaluates only cells whose raw amount already passes the surface-specific visibility cutoff.

The previous three-by-three candidate expansion and `unordered_set` construction are removed.

Smoothing remains local to each occupied cell, so the halo suppression rule remains intact.

### Phase 6 field resolution

The active Phase 6 harness changes from 42 to 32 field cells per world unit. Earlier phase harnesses preserve their original resolutions.

This reduces the approximate cell count per equal-area deposit to about 58 percent of the previous value while making a one-cell drip trace slightly wider in world space.

### Lower-frequency maintenance

The 120 Hz physics accumulator remains in place for moving droplets and runner motion, but slower field processes are decoupled:

- radial pool relaxation: 30 Hz;
- drying and freezing maintenance: 20 Hz;
- puddle-cell statistics: 4 Hz;
- dense-source scan interval: 0.30 s.

These rates use accumulated elapsed time rather than discarding time between passes.

## Success criteria

BS-015 is successful if:

- the direct renderer no longer allocates an expanded per-frame candidate set;
- Phase 6 uses 32 cells per world unit while older phases remain unchanged;
- one second of 120 Hz updates produces approximately 30 pool passes and 20 drying passes;
- empty-cell halo suppression remains valid;
- world droplets and runner motion remain on the 120 Hz fixed substep;
- the full native regression suite passes.
