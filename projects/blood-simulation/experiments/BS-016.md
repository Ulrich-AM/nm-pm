# BS-016 - Sparse Impact Drip Streaks

**Phase:** 7  
**Status:** Implemented  
**Milestone:** `blood-simulation-v0.8.0`  
**Date:** 2026-10-20

## Question

Can a fresh wall splatter occasionally form one or two narrow downward streaks without making the whole stain flow or creating new blood mass?

## Motivation

The Phase 6 reservoir runner models the uncommon case where a localized bead continues toward the edge of a surface and can become a falling world-space droplet. Smaller splatters also benefit from a second behavior: one or two thin gravity streaks that remain attached to the wall and read as blood slowly running out of the impact body.

The two behaviors should remain distinct.

## Method

After a procedural impact is merged into the persistent field, Phase 7 may nominate zero, one, or two streaks. The decision is deterministic from impact seed and volume.

Only sufficiently large impacts on gravity-bearing surfaces are eligible.

For each streak:

1. Search a small neighborhood around the impact for a wet body-or-dense cell.
2. Prefer dense cells and candidates farther in the gravity-facing direction.
3. Keep separate streak origins at least four field cells apart.
4. Gather a bounded reservoir from a two-cell neighborhood.
5. Leave at least 86 percent of each contributing cell or the body threshold, whichever is greater.
6. Advance one field cell at a time along projected world gravity.
7. Deposit a narrow single-cell trail from the streak reservoir.
8. At sparse deterministic intervals, permit a one-cell lateral step to create a mild wiggle.
9. Stop after a bounded length or at the surface edge.
10. Return the remaining reservoir to the terminal cell as a small endpoint bead.

The streak never becomes a world-space droplet. That transition remains the responsibility of the Phase 6 runner and hanging-tip path.

## Bounds

Default Phase 7 limits are:

- maximum two streaks nominated by one impact;
- maximum twelve active streaks globally;
- 13 to 30 cells of streak length;
- approximately 24 field cells per second before deterministic speed variation;
- 0.105 target reservoir per streak;
- 0.014 terminal reserve.

## Success criteria

BS-016 is successful if:

- a qualifying deterministic impact produces no more than two streaks;
- streak capture conserves field amount plus transient reservoir;
- streak motion follows downward projected gravity;
- trail deposition reduces transient reservoir by the same amount added to the field;
- repeated execution with the same seed produces the same origins and lengths;
- active streak count never exceeds its configured cap;
- streaks remain surface-local and do not create airborne droplets.
