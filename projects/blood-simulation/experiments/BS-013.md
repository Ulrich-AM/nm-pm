# BS-013 - Halo Suppression and Surface-Response Groundwork

**Phase:** 6  
**Status:** Implemented  
**Milestone:** `blood-simulation-v0.7.0`  
**Date:** 2026-10-20

## Question

Can the pale border around smoothed blood deposits be removed without reverting to hard pixel edges, while also introducing a small surface-response layer for later material work?

## Motivation

The Phase 5 renderer expanded candidate cells around occupied blood and then sampled a smoothed field. Empty neighbors could therefore receive a nonzero smoothed amount and render as a pale translucent fringe.

## Method

Phase 6 keeps smoothing, but a rendered cell must now satisfy two conditions:

1. the raw persistent field cell contains a minimum real blood amount;
2. the smoothed sample remains above a small visibility threshold.

Smoothing can therefore shape blood inside occupied cells but cannot invent a visible empty-cell border.

The opacity curve is also made more solid:

- mist remains the least opaque zone but is darker and more substantial;
- body material is strongly opaque;
- dense material is nearly opaque.

A `SurfaceResponse` scaffold maps surface kinds to response multipliers. The first checkpoint uses it to influence ribbon width and edge-cutoff sensitivity for room walls and box surfaces.

## Success criteria

BS-013 is successful if:

- an empty raw cell does not render even when smoothing produces a nonzero sample;
- a genuinely occupied low-amount cell can still render;
- dense opacity remains greater than body opacity, which remains greater than mist opacity;
- box-side response differs from room-wall response;
- the underlying field amount and simulation state remain independent of camera orientation.
