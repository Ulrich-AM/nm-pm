# BS-018 - Thick Streak Readability Patch

**Phase:** 7 reference patch  
**Status:** Implemented  
**Milestone:** `blood-simulation-v0.8.1`  
**Date:** 2026-10-20

## Problem

The v0.8.0 short impact streak system was structurally correct but too easy to miss in the native lab.

Three factors combined:

- streak nomination was still relatively conservative;
- each trail step deposited only about 0.0032 field amount;
- the trail occupied one field cell, placing much of the visible result close to the raw render cutoff.

The result could look as if no streak formed, especially at oblique camera angles.

## Patch

The final reference patch strengthens the existing Phase 7 streak path without changing its role.

### Frequency

Default first-streak probability increases from 0.68 to 0.88. Second-streak probability increases from 0.28 to 0.46. The minimum eligible impact volume is reduced from 0.50 to 0.40.

The per-impact maximum remains two streaks.

### Reservoir

Target streak reservoir increases from 0.105 to 0.190. The gather radius increases from two to three cells, while each contributing source cell retains at least 82 percent of its amount or the body threshold.

### Trail width

Each streak step now deposits a mass-conserving lateral ribbon.

The normal case distributes one step across three cells:

- 56 percent center;
- 22 percent left;
- 22 percent right.

A subset of stronger streaks may use a five-cell profile near the source, with most material still concentrated in the center three cells.

The deposited amount is subtracted from the transient reservoir exactly as before.

### Visibility

The active streak head radius increases and uses a darker, nearly opaque color. Persistent side cells now receive enough real field mass to pass the existing raw-cell visibility cutoff in normal three-cell streaks.

## Success criteria

BS-018 is successful if:

- normal streaks deposit visible material in the center and both lateral cells;
- total field increase equals the reported deposited amount;
- the existing Phase 7 deterministic, conservation, direction, active-cap, and scale tests continue to pass;
- streaks remain capped at two per impact and do not replace the rarer full runner path.
