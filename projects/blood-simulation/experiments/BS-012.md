# BS-012 - Drip Ribbon Width and Source Continuity

**Phase:** 6  
**Status:** Implemented  
**Milestone:** `blood-simulation-v0.7.0`  
**Date:** 2026-10-20

## Question

Can narrow Phase 5 drip channels be widened into heavier liquid ribbons without adding blood mass or replacing the existing channel solver?

## Motivation

Phase 5 corrected where drips originate, but the resulting runs could still read as thin scratches. The visual reference requires fewer but heavier streaks, especially beneath dense source regions.

## Method

Phase 6 preserves Phase 5 channel transport. After a source advances, a bounded portion of the material already present in its current channel cell is redistributed laterally across the surface.

Projected gravity defines the channel direction. A perpendicular surface-local vector defines the ribbon width.

- ordinary channel cells spread into a one-cell half-width ribbon;
- stronger cells may spread into a two-cell half-width ribbon;
- nearer side cells receive more material than the outer ring;
- the center cell loses exactly the amount added to the lateral cells;
- a surface-response multiplier can reduce widening on rougher box sides.

Hanging-tip display radius is also increased by a fixed Phase 6 scale factor.

## Success criteria

BS-012 is successful if:

- a strong vertical-wall source produces material on both sides of the centerline;
- strong sources can populate the second lateral ring;
- total field amount is conserved within numerical tolerance;
- the existing Phase 5 channel, outlet, hanging-tip, pooling, and transfer tests remain valid;
- hanging tips render larger than their Phase 5 equivalents.
