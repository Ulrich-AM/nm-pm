# AI Disclosure

NM-PM uses a human-directed research workflow with substantial assistance from generative AI and machine-learning-based tools.

## AI-assisted work

Generative AI may contribute substantially to:

- implementation and code generation;
- mathematical exploration and derivation support;
- rapid prototyping;
- debugging and fault isolation;
- generation and comparison of alternative approaches;
- documentation assistance;
- experiment organization;
- research planning and technical synthesis.

## Human-directed work

The human researcher directs the research questions and concepts, provides references and visual evidence, tests the systems, evaluates results, rejects unsuitable approaches, makes design decisions, and determines the direction of each study.

## Attribution principle

NM-PM should not imply that every algorithm, derivation, implementation detail, line of code, or documentation passage was produced manually by the human researcher.

AI-generated or AI-assisted material is treated as research work product that remains subject to human evaluation, testing, revision, and rejection.

This disclosure applies repository-wide unless a study documents a more specific workflow.
